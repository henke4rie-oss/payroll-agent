Penny

Payroll assistant · SARS 2026/27

How Penny works

  1. The model reads your question. It picks a tool and fills in what you asked for.
  2. Code does the maths. Every rand comes from a tested tax engine using the SARS tables, never from the model.
  3. You approve every change. Nothing is saved until you click Approve, and approved changes go to an audit log.

Demo data. Your changes are saved to a private copy that is thrown away after a while, so you can try anything.
